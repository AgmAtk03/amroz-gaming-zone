FAQ

Usual questions

Hours, walk-ins, passes, food, ages, parking, shop speed.

What can I play at Amroz?+

PlayStation 5 booths with current sports, action, and co-op titles, plus a small PC arena for competitive shooters. Game list rotates — ask the desk what’s loaded tonight.

What are your hours?+

Sunday–Thursday 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Friday and Saturday we stay open until 12:30 AM. Last booth start is 60 minutes before close.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?+

Walk-ins are welcome. Friday nights and tournament days fill up — WhatsApp or use the booking form to hold a booth. We hold a reservation for 15 minutes past the slot.

How does pricing work?+

Walk-in is hourly (see the rate strip on this page). Weekly and monthly memberships bundle hours + snack credits at a lower effective rate. Tournaments may have a separate entry fee.

Is there a membership?+

Two layers: Amroz Members is a free list (email + optional WhatsApp) with sample shop/booth perks — DEMO, stored on your device. Booth passes are Weekly Pulse, Monthly Squad, and Weekend Spark. Hours don’t roll over.

Is food and drink allowed?+

Yes. We run a small snack and cold-drink counter. Outside food is okay if it stays off the hardware. No hookah on the demo menu — ask the floor if that changes.

Do you host tournaments or birthdays?+

Weekly Tekken Fridays, seasonal FC cups, and private booth blocks for birthdays or office squads. Book 48 hours ahead for groups of 6+.

Is it okay for younger players?+

Daytime is all-ages with a guardian for guests under 13. After 9 PM we keep the floor 16+ unless a parent stays. Rated-M titles stay on marked booths.

Is there parking?+

Street parking around Pepsicola / the football ground. Bike spots in front when the shutter’s up. Exact bay count is still a demo placeholder.

How do I get in touch?+

WhatsApp +977 980-123-4567 (demo number), the form on this page, or walk up to the desk. We typically reply within an hour during open hours.

How fast are top-ups and gear?+

Digital codes and top-ups: same-day / within 2 hours in Kathmandu. Fantech gear: same-day delivery or pickup at Pepsicola / football ground — not a 2-hour window on hardware. Checkout on this site is DEMO only.

Can I buy a mouse or Free Fire top-up?+

Yes — five Fantech SKUs and seven digital hubs (Free Fire, PUBG, MLBB, Valorant, Roblox, PSN, Steam USD). Owner-pitch checkout is mock Khalti/eSewa. WhatsApp Confirm is the fallback.
